FAQ

Common questions, answered

Are hallucinogens addictive?
Classic psychedelics like LSD rarely cause physical dependence, but ketamine and PCP can drive compulsive use — and any hallucinogen can become psychologically entangled with escape and avoidance.
What is HPPD?
Hallucinogen Persisting Perception Disorder — ongoing visual disturbances (halos, trails, static) and flashbacks that persist after use stops. It’s treatable with professional support.
Aren’t psychedelics used in therapy now?
Clinical research uses screened patients, controlled doses, and trained clinicians in monitored settings. Recreational use has none of those safeguards and carries real risks.
Can one bad trip cause lasting problems?
For some people, yes — particularly anxiety, panic symptoms, or perceptual changes. These respond well to treatment.
When does hallucinogen use need treatment?
When use is escalating, interfering with daily life, worsening mental health, or you’ve tried to stop and couldn’t. A confidential assessment is the easiest way to find out.
