A Common Question

Hallucinogens vs. therapeutic psychedelics

You may have seen headlines about psilocybin or ketamine in clinical settings. Research into medically supervised psychedelic-assisted therapy is real — but it involves screened patients, pharmaceutical-grade compounds, controlled doses, and trained clinicians in a monitored environment.

Recreational use has none of those safeguards: unknown dosing, unscreened risk factors, no clinical support during distressing experiences, and street supplies that may be misrepresented or contaminated. The existence of clinical research doesn’t make unsupervised use safe — and if your use has become something you can’t step away from, that’s worth taking seriously.
