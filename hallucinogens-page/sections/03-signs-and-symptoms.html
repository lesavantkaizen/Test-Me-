Signs & Symptoms

Signs of hallucinogen use

Physical Symptoms
  • Dilated pupils
  • Elevated heart rate and blood pressure
  • Impaired coordination
  • Numbness or detachment (dissociatives)
  • Nausea
Psychological Symptoms
  • Visual or auditory distortions
  • Altered sense of time
  • Confusion or disorientation
  • Panic or paranoia during “bad trips”
  • Detachment from reality
Behavioral Symptoms
  • Impaired judgment and risky behavior
  • Long unexplained absences
  • Declining performance
  • Using in escalating amounts (ketamine, PCP)
  • Withdrawal from usual activities
