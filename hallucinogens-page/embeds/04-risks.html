Risks of hallucinogen misuse

The biggest dangers aren't always during the trip — some arrive after:

Impaired Judgment

Accidents and dangerous behavior while perception is distorted are the most immediate risk.

HPPD

Hallucinogen Persisting Perception Disorder — visual disturbances and flashbacks that continue long after use stops.

Panic Reactions

"Bad trips" can produce overwhelming fear, paranoia, and lasting anxiety.

Psychosis in Vulnerable Individuals

Hallucinogens can trigger or worsen psychotic disorders in people with personal or family risk factors.

Dissociative Harms

Heavy ketamine use damages the bladder and urinary tract; PCP is linked to violent agitation and injury.

Life Disruption

Escalating use patterns crowd out relationships, work, and daily functioning.
