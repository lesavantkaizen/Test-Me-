How Launch To Wellness Can Help

Structured care, matched to your needs

Recovery from hallucinogen use works best with the right intensity of support. Our clinical team recommends a level of care based on a confidential assessment — and adjusts it as you progress.

PHP
Partial Hospitalization

Structured daytime treatment offering comprehensive therapeutic support while clients return home in the evenings.

IOP
Intensive Outpatient

Multiple therapy sessions each week — in person or virtual — with flexibility for work, school, or family life.

OP
Outpatient Services

Ongoing support focused on relapse prevention, recovery maintenance, and continued personal growth.
