Withdrawal & Recovery

Withdrawal is safer — and easier — with support

Most classic hallucinogens don't cause physical withdrawal, but stopping heavy use — especially ketamine or PCP — can bring cravings, low mood, anxiety, sleep problems, and lingering perceptual changes.

Treatment focuses on psychological recovery: stabilizing anxiety and mood, addressing HPPD symptoms when present, and treating the underlying reasons use became compulsive.
